DESCRIPTION OF SHIPS

PURSUIT CRUISERS

The Pursuit Cruisers are an attempt to
create ships capable of catching up with
and neutralising the heretical cruisers
that come to plunder the Imperium. This
need, of course, stems from the ever-pre-
sent threat of the Dragon’s Maw Warp
Tempest at the Sector's borders. Thus,
Pursuit Cruisers are fast ships with im-
pressive raw firepower and a high rate of
fire, placing them somewhere between a
Cruiser and a Battlecruiser. In the minds
of many admirals, they were intended to
completely replace Cruisers, giving rise
to a new generation that was both more
elegant and more powerful.

Their design is based on traditional Im-
perial Navy cruisers, but modified with
improved weaponry. Indeed, on each
variant, Geor type macrocannons turret
were installed on the spine and the prow.
It is this rapid fire weaponry which grants
them their massive firepower. However,
unlike battlecruisers, they do not need
the advanced technologies, such as long
range lance battery or improved power
relays, thus, they remained far cheaper
than these ships.

Today, these ships still operate after 4500
years of constant war, they have shown
to be useful ships that could stand the
grounds in the fiercest battles.

ARISTOCRAT CLASS

The Aristocrat class is based on the Lu-
nar Cruisers but incorporates the ra-
pid-fire Geor-model macro-cannon tur-
rets characteristic of all Pursuit Cruisers.
These follow the standard configuration.
for this type of vessel, namely two on the
ship's upper deck and a third beneath its
bow. Added to this is the Lunar’s traditio-
nal armament, namely bow torpedoes,
and batteries of Laser Spears and Mars
Mark VI Macro-cannons on the flanks.
Today, this ship is valued by its captains
for its versatility, inherited from the Lu-
nar, and the absence of critical flaws.

History

The Aristocrat class was the first of the
Pursuit Cruisers to be built, and came
into being during the last two centu-
ries of M37. However, despite the rapid
construction programme underway,
the Admiralty preferred to remain cau-
tious, Before the ships entered service,
a series of tests was carried out to verify
the proper functioning of every piece of
technology on board. Once these were
complete, the first real combat exer-
cises took place within the battle group
of the Battlefleet Hellenos, to prevent a
disaster from occurring whilst the ship
was completely isolated. And so, the
first two Aristocrats were able to take
part in their first battles during the Na-
vigators’ Crusade against the Orks.

The Battle of the Charon Pass was a to-
tal Imperial victory; the dozen or so Ork
kquizers were crushed, leaving no sur-
vivors. Better still, the two Aristocrats
earned their first laurels by annihilating
a squadron of escorts and disabling a
kruizer.

A feat that was particularly notable given
that the two ships were at the rear of a
line comprising two Battleships and 15
Cruisers. Thus, the class was officially ac-
cepted into active service and endorsed
by the entire Admiralty. Production
of subsequent ships and other classes
suddenly accelerated to meet the needs
of the ongoing Crusade.

As the Navigators’ Crusade progressed,
the major battles came to an end, the
principal enemies having been defeated
and scattered. A new phase of the war
was beginning; the focus was now on
protection rather than conquest. The
Aristocrats undertook their first pa-
trols—the very role for which they had
been designed—and revealed their first
weaknesses. Thus, the Faith Judgement
pursued an unidentified Murder Cruiser.
By channelling all its energy into its en-
gines, the Pursuit Cruiser momentarily
caught up with its opponent and unlea-
shed its full firepower. Firing at maxi-
mum rate, it outmanoeuvred its oppo-
nent and struck it several times before
the latter was finally able to escape. But
this victory was short-lived; a week later,
a Litte Ork Cruiser entered the system,
and the Captain of the Faith Judgement
realised with horror that his previous
battle had exhausted his ammunition.
The ship’s macro-cannons fell silent qui-
ckly; only the laser lances prevented the
Xenos from attempting a boarding. The
order to retreat was given, thus shame-
fully abandoning the small colony he was
protecting to its fate. This flaw would af-
fect almost all of the Pursuit Cruisers.

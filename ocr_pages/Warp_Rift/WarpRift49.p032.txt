The 17th Legion

THE WORD BEARERS

Purveyors of the will of the gods, the Word Bearers
are amongst the most prolific and influential of the
traitor Astartes, both feared and demanded for their
command of Daemonology.

Daemonic Intervention

Cruit 5 pts
Battleship 50 pts
A Ship upgraded with Word Bearers may once
per game (before rolling) declare a Special
Order to be a Daemonic Intervention. Roll
Leadership as normal, then apply the following
modified effect to the order:

Ordained Advance (All Ahead Full): The Ship
moves 4D6cm as normal, and then gains
4D6cm of further movement. This second
distance is optional, and the Ship may choose to
move as much or as little of it as desired

Warped Space (Come to New Heading): The
Ship may turn after every 5cm of movement,
and may turn up to 3 times.

Borrow Time (Burn Retros): The Ship moves up
to 2D6cm directly backwards. It may not turn
this turn.

Daemon Lance (Lock On): The Ship unleashes
Dé Lance Shots against a target in its front arc
within 60cm. These shots reduce Holofields
and similar effects to a 4+, and may re-roll
misses as normal. The ship may not fire any
other weapons on this turn.

Daemon Forge (Reload Ordnance): Ordnance
released on this turn may either double its
quantity OR its movement. If any Ordnance
remains on the field after the Ordnance Phase,
you must remove markers until you are at your
Fleet's normal capacity as usual.

Displacement (Brace for Impact): Brace for
Impact saves are taken on a 2+ instead of the
standard 4+

However, if the Ship fails the Leadership test to
go on a Daemonic Intervention Special Order,
the gods have been angered by insufficient
offerings, and punish the ship with a Critical
Damage, rolled for as normal

The 18th Legion

THE SALAMANDERS

Hailing from a world of fire and ash, the
Salamanders are famed for their love of incendiary
devices, as well as their resistance to heat.

Blood of Vulcan
Cruiser.
Battleship.
A Ship upgraded with Salamanders is immune
to the Fire Critical Damage - results of 7 on
Critical Damage instead count as results of 1

The 20th Legion

THE ALPHA LEGION

Unkowable in their purpose, with schemes that span
centuries, and opperations that crumble empires
from within, the Alpha Legion is as mysterious as it
is perfidious across the galaxy.

Covert Operation +30 pts
Once per game, a Hit & Run Raid (from
Teleport Attacks or Ordnance) from a Ship
upgraded with Alpha Legion can replace one of
these Hit & Run Raids with a Covert
Opperation. The Hit & Run Raid is rolled on
2D6 as for Critical Damage caused from enemy
weapons, instead of the normal D6. This roll
does not benefit from the bonuses usually
applicabale to Space Marine Hit & Run Raids.

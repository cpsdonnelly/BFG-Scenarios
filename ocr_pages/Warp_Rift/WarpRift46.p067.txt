Light scouting cruisers like the Dauntless class have always been a feature of Imperial fleets throughout the Imperium. An ideal
light cruiser carries enough firepower to drive off opposing escorts and enough fuel and supplies to remain away from bases for
months at a time. The Dauntless is a popular class, as fast and manoeuvrable as a frigate but with a ferocious frontal lance armament.
During the Gothic War, the light cruisers Uziel and Baron Surtur were the first to detect Warmaster Khuzor's fleet near the Formosa
cluster and their accurate information allowed Admiral Sartus to bring the foe to battle.

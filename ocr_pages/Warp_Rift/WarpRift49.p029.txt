LEGIONS ASTARTES

The present document is a fan-made
supplement for the game Battlefleet Gothic
REMASTERED. It is intended to provide
additional options for the Space marine and
Chaos player who looks to find more flavour
and variety in their BFG games by offering
Legion-Specific upgrades that reflect the
character of the Adeptus Astartes across the
galaxy.

This is a small homebrew project born
from the love I have of the Adeptus Astartes -
not as valiant warriors or infamous ruffians,
but as a nuanced and poetic narrative of
brotherhood, betrayal, valour, and strife; and
for the game Battlefleet Gothic, which has
brought me much joy and great friendships
through the years. Any feedback is welcome
(through the Battlefleet Gothic: Remastered
Discord group). Thank you in advance for
reading, and I hope you enjoy what you will
find in the pages to come!

THE SPACE MARINE LEGIONS

Whether incorruptible servants of the god-emperor, fighters emancipated from the
darkness of his lies, or blood-crazed maniacs waging war in the name of
whichever god will sanction their crusade, the Astartes Legions are as varied as
they are numerous, each bringing their own flavour of war to the stars.

USING THIS SUPPLEMENT

Any player fielding a Space Marine fleet, a
Chaos fleet, or any other Fleet that can take
Space Marines as an upgrade for their ships can
look through the following pages and find a
specific legion whose talents they wish to bring
aboard their vessel. While, true to Space Marine
tradition, most of these upgrades concern Hit &
Run Attacks, or boarding actions, some are
more dynamic in their nature, displaying the
varied talents of the legions, especially those
that have adopted more esoteric or lateral
forms of war.

The seasoned Warhammer player will notice
that this supplement contains 18 upgrades
correlating to the Legions of the First Founding,
but no specific limitation on which can be used
by which fleets. This is because, as far as this
supplement is concerned, there are no such
limitations - a Space Marine Fleet can choose
upgrades from traitor legions as simply as the
Chaos fleet can choose upgrades from the
loyalist legions. This is because the fact of the

Over the millennia that followed, the
Battlefleet Hellenos remained a powerful
force, yet it would never again experience
such a glorious chapter in its history. At
times, echoes of that two-century-long
era of glory rekindled ambitions for
grand projects, but none of these achie-
ved even a tenth of the scale of that by-
gone era. Stagnation took hold of the
Battlefleet Hellenos, becoming increa-
singly pronounced, and orthodox doc-
trines became firmly entrenched.

M42 CICATRIX MALE-
DICTUM ERA

The opening of the Cicatrix Maledictum
plunged the Imperium into chaos, and
the Hellenos Sector was no exception.
The heretical offensive that followed pu-
shed the Battlefleet Hellenos to the li-
mits of its capabilities; at the same time,
it had to cope with the loss of its ships,
re-establish communications and form
an effective front against the heretical
vessels. The Sancti perfectly embodies
this critical situation; swallowed by the
Warp as soon as the Cicatrix Maledictum
opened, it has been sighted in multiple
locations simultaneously, sometimes
as a daemonic vessel, sometimes sa-
ving isolated worlds from destruction.

Despite this, with reinforcements from
the Indomitus Crusade, the Adeptus
Mechanicus and the Ministorum, the of-
fensive by the minions of ruin was halted,
so much so that the Imperial Navy has
now embarked on a protracted war to
protect the worlds still under its control
and liberate those under occupation.
What is certain is that never in its history
has the Battlefleet Hellenos played such
a vital role in the defence of the Sector.

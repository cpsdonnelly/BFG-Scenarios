THE HELLENOS SECTOR

The Hellenos Sector borders the Cicatrix Maledictum to the north of the T’au Empire in

the Segmentum Ultima. Situated in a remote area, it has always been a wealthy region

but one under constant attack, meaning it must devote the majority of its resources to its

defence. The Sector is chiefly known as the home of the Sanguine Blades, House Cilikos
and numerous Forge Worlds.

The Hellenos Sector comprises 8
Sub-Sectors, with a claimed total of
300 worlds. Occupied by a powerful
alliance of Forge Worlds, the Geor's
Tryptic, these worlds grant it re-
markable industrial might, without
which the Sector would likely have
succumbed millennia ago. Indeed,
facing it lies the Warp Storm of the
Dragon’s Maw, a terrible threat that
has been spewing forth heretical ar-
mies since the Horus Heresy. As if
that were not enough, only a narrow,
stable Warp route connects it to the
rest of the Imperium, and this is un-
der constant threat from the Xenos.
‘Thus, despite its apparent might, the
Sector survives only thanks to a pre-
carious balance between humanity
and its enemies.

Roditene, Nulna VII and Medio XII
together form the Adeptus Mecha-
nicus alliance of the Sector; thanks
to them and their vassals, they sup-
ply the entire Sector with weapons.
Although relations between Ter-
ta and this alliance may have been
strained, today the pressing needs of
the M42 are forcing a compromise.

The Imperial Knight house Cilikos is one
of the strongest military force in the whole
Sector. Its seat is located in the Equiteium
Subector to which the house swore an oath
of protection long millenias ago in exchange
for resources. Thus house Cilikos kept its so-
vereignty over these lands.

Great Crusade

The Sector was discovered fairly quickly by
an unknown legion; the Forge Worlds of the
newly created Hellenos Sector rallied to the
Imperium without hesitation and soon put
these new forces to good use, with the result
that the Sector soon saw the founding of new
Forge Worlds arriving directly from Mars.
When the Horus Heresy broke out, the Dark
Mechanicum revealed itself and the young
Forge Worlds rebelled against the Impe-
rium; thus, cut off from all else, the Helle-
nos Sector witnessed the outbreak of a fra-
tricidal war between the two factions of the
Mechanicum. Eventually, the ancient Forge
Worlds emerged victorious many years after
Horus’s death, and the Imperium reclaimed
the Sector.

Moirae Schism

When the Moirae Schism occurred, the
Sector was torn apart. However, order
was swiftly restored and the rule of the
Triptych of Geor re-established over the
core of the Hellenos Sector. Indeed, the
three Forge Worlds supported Moirae
and her dissident ideas and consoli-
dated their positions, yet the periphe-
ries of the Hellenos Sector continued to
follow Mars’s line and waged war against
the forces of the Mechanicus. Whilst
within the Imperium, Moirae’s followers
were rapidly losing ground, a Ministo-
rum Conformity Fleet swiftly arrived
in the Hellenos Sector. The Geor Trip-
tych then held council, and faced with
the imminent threat, decided to switch
sides. Thus began the Apostasy in the
Hellenos Sector.

Apostasy

During this long period, the Sector came
under the rule of the Ministorum, and
the Forge Worlds, the former masters
of these lands, reluctantly submitted
to the new order. For a millennium,
the reign of Goge Vandire dismantled
all influence of the Mechanicus over
the Hellenos Sector and launched nu-
merous crusades aimed at conquering
new territories. Shortly before Vandire’s
downfall, the Triptych of Geor rose up
against this oppression, which they dee-
med unjust. Fortunately, the tyrant fell
a few years later, offering salvation to
the Forge Worlds, who would otherwise
have inevitably lost this war; yet, despite
the tyrant’s downfall, the Mechanicus
never regained its former influence, and
Imperial power retained firm control
over the majority of the Hellenos Sector.

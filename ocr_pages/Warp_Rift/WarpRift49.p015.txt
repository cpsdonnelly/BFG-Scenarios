Ordnance

Any ship with launch bays may choose to have
them launch any mix of Fury interceptors and
Starhawk bombers. Some ships may carry
Shark assault boats at an additional cost. Ships
with torpedo tubes are armed with ordinary
torpedoes. Ships may replace their Starhawks
for Sopless bombers for +5 pts per launch bay.

Sopless

Sopless are 15cm speed resilient (4+ save) bom-
bers. Once in a game, a ship may use them as
torpedo bomber, when doing so, unlike regular
torpedo bombers, they are replaced by stren-
gth 3 torpedo salvo instead of 2.

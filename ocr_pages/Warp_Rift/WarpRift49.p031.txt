The 9th Legion

THE BLOOD ANGELS

Afflicted by a terrible darkness of the mind that
eventually turns an honourable warrior into a
mindless beast, the Blood Angels waste no potential,
unleashing their enraged brothers upon the foe to
wreack unspeakable havoc before their demise.

Unleash the Death Company
Cruiser/Escort
Battleship
Once per game, a Ship upgraded with Blood
Angels may choose to ‘Unleash the Death
Company’ when performing a Teleport Attack
or Boarding Action. Immediately perform D6
Hit & Run Raids against the enemy ship (or one
enemy ship of your choosing for multiple-ship
Boarding). If it was a Teleport Attack, it ends
there. If it was a Boarding Action, carry out the
Boarding Action as normal after Unleashing
the Death Company

The 13th Legion

THE ULTRAMARINES

Authors of the Codex Astartes, the Ultramarines
may seem pompous and prideful, especially as they
praise their deeds and primarch above other Legions.
Nevertheless, the Ultramarines have earned their
renown by demonstrable skill and valour through
countless wars and calamities.

Unbroken Chain
Escort Squadron...
Capital Ship

The firs time each ship or squadron upgraded
with Ultramarines fails a Command Check to
go on Special Orders, instead of ending the
Special Orders Phase, it may carry on as
normal. Note that the Ultramarines Ship is still
without Special Orders - they merely spare the
rest of the Fleet from having no Special Orders.

The 14th Legion

THE DEATH GUARD

The dutiful sons of the Father of Plagues, eve
contact with the Death Guard can plant the seeds of
dread malady, burning like a cancer even through
Plasteel, Wraithbone, and Necrodermis,

The Rust Plague
Escort.
Capital Ship
A Boarding Action or Hit & Run Raid (from
Teleport Attacks or Ordnance) from a Ship
upgraded with Death Guard will automatically
impart the 'Infected' status on the enemy ship.
Infected Ships suffer a standard Hit & Run raid
in each End Phase after rolling for Repairs. If at
any point a ship has no Critical Damage on it, it
immediately removes the Infected condition.
Note that Hit & Run Attacks caused by the
Infection represent the illness spreading
through the ship, and thus do not benefit from
the usual +1 modifier for a Space Marine Hit &
Run Raid.

The 15th Legion

THE THOUSAND SONS

Cunning servants to the Master of Fates, the
Thousand Sons are the most powerful sorceres of the
Astartes legions, casting terrible spells and powerful
wards that tilt the battle in their favour

Arcane Wards

Ship upgraded with Thousand Sons always
benefits from a 5+ save against enemy Lance
Hits on its Hull (not against Hits taken on
Shields). This save is upgraded to 3+ when the
Ship is on Brace for Impact Special Orders.
Note this does not apply to other sources of
Lance hits, such as Catastrophic Damage from
destroyed Ships.

The Heirs of the 16th

THE BLACK LEGION

Born from the broken remnants of the once proud
Sons of Horus, the Black Legion is as varied and
pragmatic as its eclectic composition, drawing in
members from a plethora of legions, chapters, and
warbands to form a bultwark that time and again has
brought the Imperium to the brink.

Lords of the Damned
Escort
Cruiser.
Battleship.
A Ship upgraded with the Black Legion may
choose to have any Legions Astartes upgrade
listed in this supplement. You may make this
decission in the middle of the battle as your
needs arise, even choosing an Upgrade that
should have activated earlier in the battle had
you chosen it earlier (such as Arcane Wards,
Close the Fist, etc.). Legion Upgrades that have
a one-time use (Covert Opperation, Unleash
the Death Company, etc.) are the exception to
this, and must be selected and declared at the
beginning of the battle. However, a Ship
upgraded with the Black Legion loses its
Legion bonus as soon as it becomes crippled,
and may not use a one-time ability it failed to
use before becoming Crippled. Note that once a
Ship has chosen a Legion rule, that rule remains
in play for the rest of the battle, or until the ship
becomes Crippled.

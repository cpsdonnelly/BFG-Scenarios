War of the Beast

Whilst the emergence of The Beast
posed the greatest threat to Terra, this
galactic war prompted hundreds of
Ork Warbosses to follow his example.
The Hellenos Sector was not spared.
Driven to the Sector’s periphery by the
armies of the Adeptus Mechanicus, the
Orks had licked their wounds and mul-
tiplied. But internal conflicts were no
longer enough to sate the Xenos’ thirst
for violence, and having grown stron-
ger and more cunning, they marched
from the East towards Nulna VII.

During this conflict, the Adeptus Mecha-
nicus was so desperate that it had to call
upon the Battlefleet Hellenos for aid,
which came to the rescue of the Geor's
Tryptic. This intervention earned the
Battlefleet Hellenos promises of assis-
tance that would materialise centuries

later.

Expansion

As the Adeptus Mechanicus declined,
the Adeptus Terra grew in strength, and
with it came the expansion of the Battle-
fleet Hellenos. Little by little, the Impe-
tial Navy began to exact payment for its
assistance during the War of the Beast,
particularly from Roditéne. The last rem-
nant of this debt is today the battleship
Emperor's Track, but a dozen other ships
of the line were built to honour this
contract.

This period also marks a new era for the
Imperial Navy. Indeed, the old classes
of the Great Crusade are gradually fal-
ling into oblivion as the technologies
required for their construction slip from
common knowledge and fade into obs-
curity. Gradually, new classes emerged,
whilst the losses became irreplaceable.

M35 NOVA TERRA IN-
TERREGNUM

Nova Terra marks a turning point in the
Hellenos Sector. Imperial authority is
growing stronger, much to the displea-
sure of the Geor Tryptic, particularly Ro-
diténe. With Roditéne becoming increa-
singly uncooperative, tensions rose all
the more rapidly as the Moirae Schism
spread its ideas and the prospect of eli-
minating Martian interference. At the
end of this period, this separatist cocktail
erupted on Roditéne. The Forge-Wor-
Id declared war on the Imperium. The
Battlefleet Hellenos found itself fighting
against Roditene’s ships, whilst Nulna
VII and Medio XII remained in the rear.
For 30 years, the Admirals of the Impe-
tial Navy fought their counterparts of the
Adeptus Mechanicus despite mounting,
losses, But Roditéne could easily replace
its losses, unlike the Battlefleet Hellenos,
which ended up on the brink of annihi-
lation despite the damage inflicted on
its adversary. Reduced to a quarter of its
strength, only the arrival of the Ministo-
rum brought this war to an end and sa-
ved what remained of the fleet.

M36 APOSTASY

The Apostasy was a period of intense in-
ternal strife for the Battlefleet Hellenos.
Several factions emerged and clashed
violently amongst themselves. Firstly,
the elders, having experienced the war
against Roditene, developed a strong re-
sentment towards the Forgeworlds but
remained wary of the influence of the
Ministorum; the latter embodied the
ideal of a powerful and glorious Impe-
rium, supported by its noble elites.

Opposing them was the so-called ‘Ma-
gos’ faction, made up of younger gene-
rations; they had rubbed shoulders with
the more pacifist Forge Worlds of Nulna
VII or Medio XII during their careers, or
served aboard ships built by the Mecha-
nicus; in short, they remained close to
the Adeptus Mechanicus and its inte-
rests. Finally, the camp of the Ministo-
rum’s disciples, driven by the ambition
to completely dominate the political are-
na of the Imperial Navy, sought to bring
the Forge Worlds to heel, or even destroy
them, and further establish Vandire’s au-
thority.

These three factions were at odds with
one another, each detesting the other
two, yet ready to ally with any of them
should that faction gain the upper
hand. This internal conflict persisted
throughout the entire Apostasy, inclu-
ding when the Tryptic of Geor openly
rebelled. Thus, these internal wars com-
pletely paralysed the Battlefleet Hellenos
during the conflict, and it only emerged
from its lethargy to find that Goge Van-
dire’s reign had come to an end. In the
shock of this revelation, the Sector Go-
vernor executed the leaders of each camp
and ensured that a faction of moderates
took definitive control of the Fleet. From
now on, the Admirals would only oppose
one another on strategic matters, a sub-
ject just as tense but one that would no
longer lead to civil wars of this magni-
tude.

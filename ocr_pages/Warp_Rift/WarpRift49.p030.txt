Legions Astartes by Ianuchu

Any ship upgraded with a Space Marine Crew
(or with one as default) may take one of the
following Legion Upgrades for the listed cost.
Escorts, Cruisers, and Battleships may have
different costs as indicated, or may have costs
indicated by other factors (such as number of
Launch Bays). For Defenses, use the Escort cost
for Defenses with only 1 Hit-Point, Battleship
cost for Defenses with 10+ Hit-Points, and the
Cruiser cost for every other Defense. If a ship's
class is not listed, it cannot take the upgrade.

The 1st Legion

THE DARK ANGELS

Valiant knights, peerless swordsmen, and heroes of
a thousand battles throughout the millenia, the Dark
Angels plague the nightmares of all Astartes for
their ruthless skill at dealing death upon their
brothers, loyal or otherwise.

Righteous Fratricide

. +10 pts

A Ship upgraded with Dark Angels nullifies
any special rules that would normally apply to
Hit & Run Raids made against it (this includes
any Legions Astartes upgrades from this list the
Hit & Run raid would benefit from, as well as
special bonuses such as would be gained by the
Dark Eldar Fleet, the Tyranid Fleet, etc.). Put
simply, every Hit & Run raid against this Ship
is carried out as though it was mundane
Imperial Navy Hit & Run Raid. Note that the
ship itself retains its Space Marine bonuses
against the Raid as normal

The 3rd Legion

THE EMPEROR'S CHILDREN

Willing slaves to She who Thirsts; addicts of the
most depraved sensations; and warriors of pedi
untouchable, the Emperor's Children launch
themselves into battle with no regard for self-
preservation, allowing them to score deep wounds in
their foes, even as they are cut down

The Extacy of War... +15 pts
A Ship upgraded with Emperor's children
gains a +1 bonus to the Critical Damage Roll at
the resolution of a Boarding Action. If the ship
lost the boarding action, it can always cause
Critical Damage on its foe on a roll of at least
6+

The 4th Legion

THE IRON WARRIORS

Experts of siege and famed warp-smiths of the Eye of
Terror, the Iron Warriors are amongst the most
powerful and fearsome of the traitor Legions,
bringing millenia of expertise to the battlefields of
the 41st millenium.

The 5th Legion

THE WHITE SCARS

Rapacious and ferocious riders that specialise in fast
and merciless strikes, the White Scars follow the
tennets of speed to act, speed to move, and speed to
win, often breaking their opponents before they've
had a chance to react.

Breakneck Speed....... +10 pts per Launch Bay
Thunderhawk Assault Boats launched from a
ship upgraded with White Scars gain +5cm to
their movement.

The 6th Legion

THE SPACE WOLVES

Fierce brawlers and uncompromising warriors, the
Space Wolves often seem crude and even primitive
in their approach, but their martial prowess is
beyond question.

Wreck and Plunder (apply both if applicable)

+10 pts per Launch Bay
5 pts per Boarding Torpedo Strength
Hit & Run Raids conducted by Ordnance
Waves launched by a Ship upgraded with
Space Wolves cause an additional +1 damage
on the target if they score one or more doubles
(after modifiers) when rolling for Critical
Damage.

Example 1: The Dagger-Fang launches a Boarding
torpedo salvo with a strength of 6, 4 of which strike
their target. When rolling the Hit & Run attacks,
the Space Wolves player rolls 2, 3, 3, 6 after
modifiers. As two of the critical results rolled the
same number, the Salvo inflicts an additional +1
Damage on the target.

Example 2: A massive Ordnance wave consisting of
10 Thunderhawks barrels through the void at their
target. 2 are shot down by Turrets, leaving 8 of them
to conduct Hit & Run Raids. The Space Wolve
player rolls 2, 3, 4, 4, 4, 5, 5, 6. Note that, et
though two numbers were rolled more than onc
and even though one was rolled 3 times, each
Ordnance wave or Torpedo Salvo can only cause +1
additional damage on the target as a results of the
Space Wolves upgrade, and thus only +1 additional
damage is applied.

The 7th Legion

THE IMPERIAL FIST

Masters of fortifications, stern defenders, and
unbending in their loyalties, the Imperial Fist have
protected the Imperium against innumerable foes.

Close the Fist . +5 pts per Turret
A Ship upgraded with Imperial Fists may re-
roll all Turrets fire against enemy Ordnance.
Furthermore, each Ship can contribute two of
its Turrents to another ship's defense instead of
the usual one. Note the combined Turret
maximum remains capped at +5.

The 8th Legion

THE NIGHT LORDS

Vicious and wicked, the Night Lords have long stood
amongst the most infamous and detested of legions
amongst the stars for their gruesome fear-tactics.

Unspeakable Horror 1 #30 pts
Hit & Run Raids from a Ship upgraded with
Night Lords, or from its Ordnance, apply an
additional -1 penalty to an enemy ship's
Leadership if they roll at least a 4+ after
modifiers. This penalty is not cummulative (i.e.
each ship can be affected by this only once), but
it remains for the rest of the game and cannot be
repaired. Eldar Ghost Ships, Tyranid Hive
Ships and Necron ships can be affected by this
upgrade as normal - we simply presume the
Night Lords have learned to disrupt Tyranid
neural pathways, or disturb Eldar infinity
circuits, or cripple Necron comman nodes.

BATTLEFLEET HELLENOS

From the vast expanses of space in the Racivia Extrema Sub-Sector to the teeming centres
of the Macedia Sub-Sector, the Hellenos Battlefleet is ever vigilant. Its frigates patrol the
void tirelessly, whilst its battleships will never cease to stand between the Worlds and
the invaders who threaten them. A true guardian of the Sector's prosperity, the Hellenos
Battlefleet brilliantly embodies the traditions of the Imperial Navy.

The Hellenos Battlefleet has a long and eventful history, to the extent that no single doctrine has ever
dominated throughout its entire history. To make the archivist’s task even more difficult, the Hellenos
Battlefleet has often provided fertile ground for doctrinal innovations, at least by the standards of the
Imperium.

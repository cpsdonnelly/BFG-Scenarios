#### The Road to Hell (`adv-road-to-hell`)

*BFG Advanced Scenarios, PDF p.6. Author: Paolo Barbero (revised from AdeptiCon tournament missions). Type: tournament/matched. Generic: yes. Factions: any. In booklet: no. OCR: yes (numbers and titles checked against the page image at 110 dpi). Diagram: simple. Rules text: about 0.5 page.*

Two semi-stable warp rifts lie 30cm either side of the table centre. Fleets deploy alternately within 30cm of the long edges. Ships fully within 20cm of a rift at the end of their movement may 'board' it to deploy a Rift Stabilizer; the action always succeeds.

**Forces.** Up to 1250 points per fleet (framework p.5), official fleet list, at least one cruiser or battleship.

**Special rules.** Deploy Rift Stabilizers: boarding action against a warp rift from within 20cm, always successful (escort squadrons count as a multi-ship boarding action).

**XR and booklet rules relied on.** s.32 Boarding Actions; s.39 Warp Rifts; s.37 Disengaging; s.38 Victory Points; Scenarios: Using Attack Ratings.

**Conflicts with XR.**

- **Attack rating roll.** XR (Scenarios, Using Attack Ratings (XR p.78)): “have each player roll a number of D6 equal to their fleet's attack rating and choose the best individual dice score” Effect: The source rolls one die and adds the attack rating. Under XR the attack rating is the number of dice. The two methods favour high-rating fleets very differently (a Corsair Eldar fleet adds 4 to one die instead of taking the best of four).
- **Tournament framework.** Source: “Sub-plots, Adeptus Mechanicus' Quest for Knowledge included, are not active.” XR (Scenarios, Sub-Plots (XR p.80)): “each player (starting with the attacker) may choose whether or not to generate a sub-plot.” Effect: The framework (1250pt limit, Battle Points, no sub-plots) is a tournament layer outside XR. Outside a tournament the supplement would need a decision on whether to keep it.
- **Admiral, Flagship and Bridge Smashed.** Source: “If the ship an Admiral is appointed to suffers a Bridge Smashed critical hit, the Admiral counts as having been killed for all Secondary Objectives tied the Admiral's demise or survival.” XR (s.5 Fleet Commanders; booklet ruling on Bridge Smashed): “Fleet commander re-rolls are lost if the commander's ship suffers Bridge Smashed or is destroyed.” Effect: Compatible in spirit. XR calls the character a fleet commander, not an Admiral. The framework applies to the Secondary Objectives only; VP for the commander's ship follow s.38 as normal.
- **Boarding a warp rift.** Source: “ships fully within 20 cm of a Warp Rift are allowed to declare a boarding action against it in order to deploy a Stabilizer” XR (s.32 Boarding Actions, Moving in): “To initiate a boarding action, the owning player must begin by moving the attacking vessel into base contact with the target and declaring the intent to board.” Effect: XR boarding needs base contact with a ship and a troop rating contest. A rift is not a ship and the source needs no contact. The source overrides the XR rule for this one use, so the supplement would state it as a special rule and drop troop ratings.
- **Warp rift size and effects.** XR (s.39 Warp Rifts): “Warp rifts are D3x5cm wide and D3x10cm long.” Effect: The source gives no rift size; the diagram shows two equal rifts. Navigation tests and destruction of ordnance follow XR unchanged.
- Checked and no conflict: first turn roll-off (s.2), alternate deployment, gas clouds and asteroid fields (s.39), VP (s.38), game length (time limit allowed by s.2 'Ending the battle').

**Missing or unclear.**

- Rift size is not stated (the diagram shows both rifts the same size).
- What a deployed Stabilizer does after deployment is not stated; it only earns +4 BP.
- 'Semi-stable': whether stabilised rifts stop being a hazard to navigation is not stated, so XR warp rift tests are assumed to continue.
- Whether a ship can board a rift it has already stabilised.

**Notes.** Two-player symmetric. Secondary Objectives (Battle Points): +4 deploy one or more Rift Stabilizers; +2 completely destroy a capital ship or escort squadron; +2 kill the enemy Admiral or the Admiral disengages. VP as normal. Original: Tournament Mission Pack A2 (AdeptiCon 2007).

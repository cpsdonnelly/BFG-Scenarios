# Content index of the fan scenario sources

One table per publication. "PDF p." is the page number in the PDF file; "printed" is the page number printed on the page, where one is visible. Types: scenario, ship design / fleet list, campaign, rules variant, tactics, Q&A/FAQ, battle report, fiction, other.

Text-layer notes: **OCR** marks a scanned file whose text is Tesseract OCR (numbers unreliable until checked against the page image). **No text** marks pages with no text layer at all; they were read from the page image.

### BFG Advanced Scenarios (Paolo Barbero) — `BFG advanced scenarios .pdf`, 12 PDF pages, OCR

Revised versions of six AdeptiCon tournament missions (the originals are in the Tournament Mission Pack below). No printed page numbers.

| Title | PDF p. | Type | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| Introduction | 3 | other | Credits; the six scenarios are revamped AdeptiCon tournament missions. |
| Advanced Rules | 5 | rules variant | Tournament framework: 1250pt fleets, at least one cruiser or battleship, official fleet lists, no sub-plots, Battle Points from the VP difference, Admiral secondary objectives, bonus VP if a fleet is gone by turn 1-4. |
| The Road to Hell | 6 | scenario | Any vs any. Two warp rifts 30cm from the centre; ships within 20cm "board" a rift to deploy a Stabilizer; secondary objectives for Battle Points. |
| An Unexpected Encounter | 7 | scenario | Any vs any. The table split into six tiles; each fleet in three detachments arriving through the game (Mandeville Point, First Contact rules). |
| Brace for Impact | 8 | scenario | Any vs any. A moon explodes; Hurtling Asteroids cross the table from a short edge each turn and split when they pass through asteroid fields. |
| Capture the Station | 9 | scenario | Any vs any. An armed Archaeotech Research Station in the centre fires at the nearest ships each turn and can only be boarded, to retrieve data-cores. |
| The Black Hole | 10 | scenario | Any vs any. A short edge is a black hole; each turn every ship is pulled xD6cm towards it (x = the game turn) and destroyed on contact. |
| Wrecks O'Plenty | 11 | scenario | Any vs any. Seven wreck tokens round a central planet, salvaged by boarding; a 1 overloads the wreck's reactor. |
| Back cover | 12 | other | Disclaimer. |
